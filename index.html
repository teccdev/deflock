UNCONSTITUTIONAL SURVEILLANCE
IN THE MIDWEST

There are currently >100,000 automated license plate readers (ALPRs) in the United States. These devices use AI to read license plates, vehicle characteristics, and more, and then automatically store them in (unsecured) databases.

These devices are supposedly used to stop crime, but independent studies have repeatedly shown that Flock cameras have no impact on crime rates, despite claims from police departments and Flock itself.

This is just the tip of the iceberg. Learn how Flock Safety is allowing government bodies to violate your constitutional rights and what you can do to stop them in your local community.

The "safety" myth

While Flock would like police departments and civilians to believe that their devices reduce crime, they don't. Less than 0.3% of hits provided any leads to crimes in a sixteen-year study in the city of Piedmont, CA, per independent.org.

This wasn't a one-off case; despite Flock's numerous flawed studies and claims, their statements have been dismantled by researchers (including Forbes and others).

Police departments have had access to tons of effective, non-intrusive technology for decades. Flock cameras aren't a miracle safety tool like they claim — they've repeatedly proven themselves to be an expensive, near-useless piece of equipment funded by you, the taxpayer.

Constitutional Rights Violations

4th Amendment

Law enforcement does not need a warrant nor probable cause to inspect your vehicle via ALPRs. Keep in mind, ALPRs like Flock's don't just check your license plate like old-school ALPRs do. They keep a precise 'fingerprint' of your car based on hundreds of pictures, using it to track where you've been and where you've been going.

They know where you work. They know where your house is. They know your doctor's office. Everything is being recorded and analyzed with AI to track you — without your consent and without you even being suspected of a crime. This is a clear violation of every US citizen's 4th amendment rights against unreasonable searches, and multiple lawsuits, like this one, are currently happening.

2nd Amendment

Don't think this is the only right they're going after — Flock also produces gunshot detectors, tracking when and where you shoot guns. Utilizing your right to bear arms on your own property is creating an AI profile of you.

...et cetera

While these are the most obvious threats to your rights, Flock is also producing drones and other AI tracking equipment. Even if everything happening so far doesn't seem too bad, the government can and will continue to track everything they can in the name of 'safety' unless we fight back.
